Categories

  • stepped squeak Several steady pitch plateaus with abrupt jumps between them (mode hopping) — #1, #2, #5, #11, #22
  • gliding squeak Mostly one continuous pitched glide, few or no jumps — #4, #21
  • squeak → creak Pitched squeak that breaks up into irregular stick-slip pulses — #0, #3, #6, #8, #13, #14, #16, #18, #20
  • creak / ratchet Mostly aperiodic: separate slip pulses, heard as a creak or ratchet — #7, #9, #12, #15, #17, #19
  • low groan Pitched but low (under ~600 Hz), often wobbly — #10
